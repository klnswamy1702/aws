# IAM Basic Interview Questions

### Q001: What is the best approach for policy types?
**Level:** L1-L2 | **Category:** conceptual/practical/troubleshooting/architecture/security/cost-optimization
**Target Services:** IAM

> **Quick Answer:** The best approach for policy types is to evaluate requirements and follow least privilege.

#### Detailed Answer
When dealing with policy types, ensure that you use IAM features effectively.
```bash
aws iam list-policies
```
